Spiral — an experimental AI film

EXPERIMENTAL AIGC VIDEO
memory · identity · media residue

A short film about how someone raised inside screens remembers themselves. Fragmented digital imagery, dream logic and layered projection turn "distorted memory" into something you can watch happen — the emotional through-line runs from low-res childhood fragments to dissolving light.

  • Make the abstract watchable. "Distorted memory" can't be filmed directly, so I broke it into a set of visual motifs — screen glare, projected folds, home footage, the fragmented body.
  • Control emotion through the frame. Rhythm, colour shift and layered overlays decide whether the viewer is pulled in or pushed away — chosen for feeling, not for looking nice.
  • The image carries its own arc. From low-resolution fragments, through repeated scrolling and staring, to a dissolve into light — a complete emotional curve, not a plot summary.
This is exactly what AI video most often gets wrong: without a motif set, prompts drift every run; with one, the style holds. That motif discipline is the foundation of world-building for AI short drama.
Method · theme to asset

From an abstract theme to prompts you can feed straight into AI

What this project really trains is translating "what I want to say" into "what goes in the frame." The endpoint of that translation is exactly the input format AI generation needs — theme tags, character, scene and shot descriptions.

01The four-step translation

  • Emotional entry — decide what the viewer arrives feeling: trauma, dislocation, being shaped.
  • Visual motif — fix that feeling into 4–6 recurring picture elements.
  • Material & light — let texture and light decide whether they're pulled in or pushed away.
  • Rhythm — order motifs along the emotional curve, not the narrative timeline.

02The prompt assets it produces

  • Theme tags — memory distortion / identity afterimage / shaped by media.
  • Character — young woman, fragmented memory, quiet but tense.
  • Scene — indoor projection, soft fabric, warm-cool mixed light, screen glare.
  • Shot — hand close-ups, slow push-in, still gaze, dream-like transitions.

Process · research to flow

From interviews to a recommendation loop that actually runs

What this proves isn't that I can draw screens — it's that I can break a cultural interest into user motivation, product mechanism and content format. That's exactly the skill behind interactive content, AI content tools and creator co-creation.

Insight

Users aren't asking for a fortune — they want a psychological anchor and a fast reason to decide in an uncertain moment. Outfit is both identity and daily content.

Mechanism

Turn birth data, body info and style preference into an element profile, then map it to colour, material and items. Explainable rules make the recommendation trustworthy.

Extensible format

The same mechanism can become an interactive test, a short-video series, a creator co-creation topic, or selection and content packaging for commerce.
